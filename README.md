One image summing up the world news of **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**,
drawn as a newspaper front page. Lead story: U.S. jets strike an Iranian
missile site on Qeshm Island after Iran fires drones at U.S. bases in
Bahrain and Kuwait, with tankers burning in the Strait of Hormuz. Also
covered: Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in Shenzhen ahead of APEC
trilateral talks with Donald Trump; Democrat Mary Peltola wins Alaska and
flips the U.S. Senate to 56–44; Nvidia posts a record $115.8B quarter;
Cyclone Akvan heads for India's east coast; and Voyager 1 becomes the
first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth. The ticker
adds Ebola passing 11,000 cases in DR Congo, the U.S. losing its
measles-elimination status after 26 years, and the Kerch Bridge road
span collapsing.

Every fact was cross-checked against at least two independent news
sources (Reuters, AP, AFP, CNN, USA Today, NASA/JPL, PAHO, WHO, IMD,
Skymet). The three illustrations are original flat editorial artwork
made for this entry; all text is drawn with real fonts, so nothing is
garbled. `build_image.py` regenerates `entry.png` deterministically
with Pillow — run it to rebuild the image, then `main.py` copies it to
the output directory.
